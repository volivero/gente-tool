A working tool for territorial prioritisation. Set criteria weights with the Best–Worst Method, then move conflict and governance between four positions in the model and watch the territorial ranking respond.

1 · Weight the suitability criteria

First select the most important criterion and rate the others against it; then select the least important and rate the others against it. The scale runs from 2 to 9. Five criteria require seven judgements.

Data. Viable areas are the published GENTE results: onshore wind from Olivero-Ortiz et al., Land 2026, 15, 923 (CC BY, doi.org/10.3390/land15060923); utility-scale solar from a companion manuscript currently under review. Expert weights are the BWM group profiles reported in those studies.

Indices. NCI and NGI values are the normalised departmental values reported in GENTE Deliverable 14, Tables 17 and 22. The NCI covers armed and humanitarian conflict (forced displacement, restrictions on humanitarian access and mobility, threats and disappearances, homicides of social leaders, and massacres); the NGI covers institutional capacity, transparency and corruption risk, planning capacity, and political stability.
