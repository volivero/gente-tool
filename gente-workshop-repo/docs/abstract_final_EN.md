# Final session abstract (sent to the organisers, 15 September 2026)

Technical siting analyses and political decisions rarely meet. This workshop connects them through an
interactive, GIS-based multi-criteria decision tool built on GENTE, a Colombian onshore wind study. In
small groups, participants weight evaluation criteria, then decide where conflict and governance belong
in the model — as interpretation, as weighted criteria, or as thresholds — and observe how the
territorial ranking changes. The exercise surfaces what an indicator measures, and for whom. No
engineering background is required; the focus is on integrating perspectives across sectors and
regions. Participants are asked to bring a laptop or tablet. The output is an annotated
decision-framework outline that can serve as the basis for a future co-authored Policy Brief.
